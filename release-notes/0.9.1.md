DiscShift gets itself and your Mac ready with less setup.

- Open your download and DiscShift installs itself in Applications before starting setup. Existing copies are protected, with a choice to replace or open them.
- One reader installation includes its DVD support, with a clear ready state when setup finishes.
- Separate DVD-support options appear only when support is still missing after checking your reader.
- Technical names, custom locations, and installation logs stay available in Advanced and Details.
- Settings now keeps everyday controls upfront and technical options tucked away.
