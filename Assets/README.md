# App icon

`AppIcon.png` is the full-resolution icon master. `scripts/build-app.sh` generates the standard macOS icon sizes and `AppIcon.icns` from this file. Artwork is covered by the repository's MIT license.

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Final prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final production macOS app icon for DiscDock, a DVD episode converter and TV-library organizer. New design. One extremely simple polished symbol: a silver optical disc on the left, seamlessly merging into one rounded video/episode tile on the right with a small simple play triangle cutout. The overlap/transition communicates taking a disc and making a watchable episode. TWO clear geometric forms maximum, no arrows, no dock, no drive, no tray, no folder, no stacks, no extra badges, no grooves or ornamental details. Restrained modern macOS dimensional style, smooth lightly frosted surfaces, soft restrained lighting. Rounded-square rich blue app tile background, disc in pearl silver and video tile in pale ice-blue. Large simple readable silhouette at 32px. Square icon centered, 8 percent transparent margin outside rounded square, transparent background, crisp edges, no text, no watermark. Show only finished icon artwork, not a device mockup. Keep it beautifully minimal and relevant to DVD-to-episodes.
